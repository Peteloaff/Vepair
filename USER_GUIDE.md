# VepAIr User Guide

This is a plain-English walkthrough of VepAIr for people actually using the app — not a
technical reference. If you're looking for setup/deployment/API details, see
[`TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md`](TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md) instead.

VepAIr is **not** a medical diagnostic device. It never tells you that you have a condition, and
it never tells you that you're "healed." It measures your own voice over time and compares it
only to *your own* past recordings — never to anyone else's. See
[`MEDICAL_SAFETY.md`](MEDICAL_SAFETY.md) for the full detail behind that rule.

## 1. Getting started

### Create an account

1. Go to the app and tap **Create an account** (not "Log in" — that's only for people who
   already have an account).
2. Enter an email and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. You're dropped straight onto the **onboarding** screen — no separate step needed.

### Onboarding

Everything here is optional. Skip anything you'd rather not answer — it only helps VepAIr
personalize things, it never gates access to the app.

- **Pick a track**: **Vocal Repair** (gentle, steadiness-focused — range suggestions pause until
  your data looks consistently stable) or **Vocal Improvement** (a step up — exercises get more
  demanding and range suggestions stretch further). This is a self-selected focus, not a
  diagnosis — VepAIr has no way to know whether you ever had a vocal injury. You can change it
  any time.
- **A few background questions**: how you mainly use your voice, whether you consider yourself a
  singer, musical style, practice frequency, your own sense of your range, your goals, and
  whether you've worked with a vocal coach or are currently under professional care.
- Tap **Save**, then **Done** to head to your dashboard.

### Forgot your password?

Go to **Forgot password?** on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset link. For
privacy, VepAIr always shows the same "a reset link is on its way" message whether or not that
email actually has an account — it never confirms or denies who has signed up.

## 2. Your dashboard

This is home base. From here you can reach **Progress**, **Vocal plan**, **Vocal range**,
**Voice exercises**, and **Record voice sample**.

### Daily check-in

A quick, entirely optional journal — every field can be skipped individually:

| Field | What it captures |
|---|---|
| Perceived voice quality (1–10) | How your voice feels today, in your own judgment |
| Fatigue (1–10) | How vocally tired you feel |
| Throat discomfort (0–10) | Any discomfort, on a scale |
| Speaking / singing load yesterday | None / Low / Moderate / High |
| Rehearsal or performance yesterday? | Yes / No |
| Sleep last night (hours) | Numeric |
| Hydration, alcohol, smoke/vape exposure | None / Low / Moderate / High |
| Illness symptoms, reflux symptoms, notes | Free text |

Once submitted, it shows as a read-only summary for the day with an **Edit** link if you need to
change it. This data feeds your **VepAIr Score** and your adaptive exercise routine — the more
consistently you check in, the more it has to work with.

### VepAIr Score

A daily 0–100 score with a GREEN / YELLOW / RED status. Tap **"why did I get this score?"** to
see the actual breakdown — which measurements contributed and how much. This is a training/
recovery indicator, not a medical score, and it's fully explainable by design: nothing about it
is a black box.

### Your vocal baseline and trends

Once you have enough sessions, the dashboard shows your personal averages and four trend charts
(voice quality, fatigue, throat discomfort, sleep) over 7/30/90 days. Everything is compared only
against your own history.

## 3. Recording a voice sample

Tap **Record voice sample**. This is a guided, ~3–5 minute session. Before you start:

- Find a quiet room.
- Hold your device at a consistent distance each time.
- Avoid touching the microphone.
- Use the same microphone across sessions if you can — consistency matters more than any single
  recording.

Tapping **Start session** asks for microphone permission — if you deny it or don't have a mic,
you'll get a clear message explaining what to do (check your browser's site settings and allow
the mic, or connect one).

**The 7 steps:**

1. Sustained **"Ah"** — hold it steady, comfortably, for as long as feels natural.
2. Sustained **"Ee"**
3. Sustained **"Oo"**
4. A comfortable hum at a natural pitch — no strain.
5. A gentle pitch glide, low to high, like a soft siren — don't push for range.
6. Read a standard sentence aloud at your normal speaking pace (the opening line of the classic
   "Rainbow Passage": *"When the sunlight strikes raindrops in the air, they act as a prism and
   form a rainbow."*).
7. **Optional, skippable**: sing a short phrase from a song you know well.

For each step you can **Retake** before committing, or **Use this take**. If a take is clipped,
too quiet, or too short, you'll see a warning — but you can still use it if you want.

At the end, you get a summary of every recording with a quality label (excellent/good/fair/poor)
and the raw numbers behind it (pitch, jitter, shimmer, HNR) — always labeled as raw acoustic
measurements, never a diagnosis.

## 4. Voice exercises

Tap **Voice exercises**. Pick how much time you have (a few preset lengths), and how often you
want live coaching feedback (Frequent / Normal / Minimal). VepAIr builds a routine from today's
check-in, your recent recordings, and your recovery status — it's different every day on
purpose.

If your recent data suggests you should go gentle, you'll see a **"Before you start"** screen
explaining the routine has been kept to the gentlest exercises only. Reported discomfort always
caps the routine — VepAIr never tells you to "push through" anything.

During each exercise you'll see the instructions, a countdown, and (mic permitting) live
coaching feedback on pitch, volume, onset, or glide smoothness — this happens entirely on your
device; nothing is uploaded for this part. You can **Skip** or **Mark done** on any exercise.
Categories span breathing, humming, lip/tongue trills, resonant voice work, straw phonation
(SOVT), sirens/glides, range exploration, and cooldown work.

At the end you'll see how many exercises you completed, per-exercise trend notes (improving/
declining), and a button to **Share My Progress**.

## 5. Vocal range mapping

Tap **Vocal range**. This is a quick, comfortable range test — never force a note, and stop the
moment anything feels strained.

1. Your comfortable **low** note.
2. Your comfortable **high** note.
3. **Optional**: a falsetto/head-voice note, if that's part of your voice.

You'll get a piano-style visualization of your current comfortable range, your historical best,
and how much it's changed over 30 and 90 days. These numbers describe your own range over time —
never a definitive classification of your voice type.

## 6. Your 90-day plan

Once you've picked a track (Repair or Improvement) **and** completed both a recording and a
vocal range test, VepAIr builds a 90-day plan specific to your own measured data — you'll find
it under **Vocal plan**. It shows your goal (stability, or range extension), a target date, and
days remaining.

If you're on the Repair track and your recent data has looked consistently stable for a while,
VepAIr automatically moves you up to an Improvement plan — this is always described as "your
data has been consistently stable," never as "you're healed." You can also switch tracks
manually any time from onboarding.

**Note:** a plan needs *both* a recent recording and a vocal range test — having just one won't
produce a plan yet. If you're missing one, the page tells you exactly which one.

## 7. Share My Progress

After an exercise session (or any time from the dashboard, once you have data), tap **Share My
Progress**. This builds two ready-to-post, vertical (1080×1920) images from your own real data —
nothing is invented, and anything you don't have data for is simply left off rather than faked:

- **Page 1 — Today's Voice**: today's snapshot.
- **Page 2 — My Progress**: a start-vs-now comparison.

Use **Previous/Next** to flip between them, then **Share** (uses your device's native share sheet
if available, otherwise downloads), **Save** (downloads the current image), or **Save Both**.

## 8. Progress dashboard

Tap **Progress** for the long view: your VepAIr Score charted over 7/30/90/180 days, 1 year, or
all-time; your current and longest daily training streak with a calendar-style activity grid; and
every exercise's trend (Improving / Declining / Stable / "Not enough data yet"). Every comparison
here is against your own history only — never a population norm.

## 9. Troubleshooting

- **"I can't sign in"** — Login only works if you already have an account. If you're not sure,
  try **Create an account** instead — signing up with an email that's already registered will
  tell you plainly, but a failed *login* deliberately shows a generic "incorrect email or
  password" message and never confirms whether an account exists, for privacy/security reasons.
- **Microphone won't work** — check your browser's site permissions for the page and make sure
  the mic is allowed; if you don't have a mic connected, recording and vocal range steps won't
  work, but the rest of the app (check-ins, exercises without live coaching, viewing progress)
  still does.
- **A page says "not enough data yet"** — that's expected early on. Most features (baseline,
  plan, trends) need a handful of real sessions before there's enough to compare against; VepAIr
  never fills gaps with invented numbers.
- **Something looks wrong or broken** — recording deletion isn't built yet, so anything you
  record stays in your history; if you hit an actual bug, report exactly what you were doing and
  what you saw.
